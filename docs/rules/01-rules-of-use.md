# Rules of use

By using AutoMate you agree to these rules.

## Be responsible
- Use AutoMate only on **your own device**, or on a device whose owner clearly agreed.
- Do not use it to track, spy on or harass other people.
- Do not build rules that spam notifications or sounds at yourself or others.

## Stay safe
- Never rely on AutoMate for **safety-critical** tasks such as medical alerts, emergency calls or alarms you must not miss.
- Do not interact with your phone while driving. Rules like "open maps when the car connects" should be set up before you drive.
- Test rules before trusting them, and keep other backups for important reminders.

## Respect the law and the platform
- Follow the laws that apply where you live.
- Do not use AutoMate to bypass the protections of Android or other apps.

## Open source behaviour
- Be kind in issues and pull requests. See the Code of Conduct on GitHub.
- Report security problems privately. See SECURITY on GitHub.

## Changes
These rules may change in future versions. The current version is always in the app under Learn.

## Social media rules
- Do not use AutoMate to spam, run bots, fake engagement or mass-message people.
- Do not use it to pressure, harass or impersonate anyone.
- Follow the terms of every platform you use. AutoMate only opens apps and links; what you post is your responsibility.
- Only create chat shortcuts to people who are fine with receiving your messages.
