# Privacy

AutoMate is built to stay on your phone.

## What AutoMate stores
- Your rules, settings, name and the last 200 activity lines.
- All of it is stored locally in the app's private storage.

## What AutoMate does not do
- It has **no internet permission**, so it cannot upload anything.
- No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
- It does not read your messages, calls, contacts, photos or microphone.

## Why each permission exists
- **Notifications**: show rule alerts and the engine notice.
- **Location (precise and all the time)**: arrive and leave rules, and reading Wi-Fi names.
- **Nearby devices (Bluetooth)**: know which device connected.
- **Display over other apps**: open apps and links from the background.
- **Exact alarms**: run time rules on the minute.
- **Run at startup**: restart the engine after a reboot.
- **Foreground service**: keep the engine alive.
- **Battery optimisation exemption**: stop the system from pausing the engine.
- **Do Not Disturb access**: allow silent ringer mode.
- **Vibrate**: the vibrate action.
- **Modify system settings**: change screen brightness.
- **Accessibility service**: optional system button presses.
- **Text-to-speech visibility**: find your phone's speech engine.
- **Network state**: notice Wi-Fi connecting and disconnecting.

## Optional Accessibility service
If you switch it on, AutoMate uses it only to press system buttons (Home, Back, Recents, notifications, quick settings, lock screen, screenshot, power menu) when a rule asks. Window content access is disabled: the service cannot read your screen or what you type.

## Other features
- **Speak text** uses your phone's text-to-speech engine. Text stays on the device unless your chosen voice engine works online.
- **Backup files** are created only when you export. They contain your rules in plain text, so store them safely.
- **Home-screen widget** shows only the engine status and your count of active rules.
- **Stats** are daily run counters stored on your phone.

## Backups
If Android backup is on for your phone, your system may include AutoMate's local data in its normal device backup.

## Deleting your data
Use **Settings > Data** in the app, or uninstall AutoMate.

## Social and sharing features
- Opening a social app, link, share sheet or WhatsApp chat only hands your text or link to the app you choose. AutoMate itself sends nothing anywhere.
- The "project link" in the Social hub is stored only on your phone.
- The clipboard action only **writes** text you typed into a rule. AutoMate never reads your clipboard.
