# Social media limits

Social apps protect their platforms and their users. AutoMate respects that.

## What AutoMate cannot do
- **Post, like, comment, follow or message for you.** AutoMate only opens the app, a profile, the share sheet or a prefilled chat.
- **Press Send or Post.** You always do the last tap yourself.
- **Read your feed, messages or notifications** from other apps.
- **Log in to your accounts.** It has no accounts and no internet permission.

## Things that depend on the app
- Profile links open inside the app only if the app is installed and supports that link. Otherwise they open in your browser.
- Apps change their links over time. If a profile link stops working, open the website version.
- WhatsApp numbers need the country code and digits only, for example `15551234567`.
- Some regions use different app versions, so a tile may open the website instead.

## Why there are no auto-post features
Bots and mass automation break the terms of most platforms and can get accounts restricted. Keeping the last tap manual keeps you in control.

## Related
- Rules of use: do not use AutoMate for spam, fake engagement or harassment.
- Android restrictions: opening apps from the background needs extra permission.
