# Social media automation

AutoMate can't post for you, but it can make your posting routine effortless.

## What you can automate
- **Reminders**: a notification at your best posting time, for example 18:00 on weekdays.
- **Shortcuts**: open Instagram, YouTube, X, TikTok, Twitch, Telegram and more at a set time, when you leave work, or when your headphones connect.
- **Profile links**: open a specific profile or channel by typing a username, for example `nasa`.
- **Share sheet**: open Android's share sheet with your text already filled in.
- **WhatsApp**: open a chat with a number and message ready. You press send yourself.
- **Caption saver**: copy a caption to the clipboard so you can paste it into any app.

## Quick examples
1. **Post reminder**: At 18:00 → notification "Time to post something!".
2. **Stream time**: At 20:00 → open Twitch.
3. **Good morning text**: At 08:00 → WhatsApp chat with "Good morning!" ready.
4. **Share my day**: At 21:00 → share sheet with "My day in one line:".

## The Social hub
Open it from Home. It has:
- Tiles to open your apps in one tap.
- A share button for AutoMate, with an optional project link.
- Ready-made social recipes you can edit and save.

## Tips
- Time rules that open apps or the share sheet need **Display over other apps**, or you will get a tap-to-open notification instead.
- Pair a reminder rule with an "open app" rule at the same time, so you get a nudge and a shortcut.

> Note: usernames work for Instagram, YouTube, X, Facebook, Telegram, TikTok, Reddit, LinkedIn, Snapchat, Pinterest, Twitch and Threads. WhatsApp and Discord open the app only.
