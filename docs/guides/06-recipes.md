# Rule recipes

Ideas you can build in a minute.

## Everyday
- **Morning hello**: At 07:30 on weekdays, send a notification with your plan.
- **Bedtime nudge**: At 22:30, notify "Time to wind down".
- **Welcome home**: When your home Wi-Fi connects, show a toast.

## Battery and power
- **Low battery**: At 15%, send a notification.
- **Charger reminder**: When the charger disconnects, vibrate once.

## Places
- **Quiet at work**: When you arrive at work, set the ringer to vibrate.
- **Loud again**: When you leave work, set the ringer to normal.

## Devices
- **Car mode**: When your car Bluetooth connects, open your maps app.
- **Headphones**: When your headphones connect, open your music app.

> Tip: duplicate the idea, not the rule. Create two rules (arrive and leave) for each place.
