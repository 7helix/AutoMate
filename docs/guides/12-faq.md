# Frequently asked questions

## Does AutoMate need the internet?
No. It has no internet permission and works fully offline.

## Does it post or message for me?
No. It can open an app, a profile, the share sheet or a prefilled chat, but you always press the final button.

## Why do I need to start the engine?
Time and repeat rules use the system alarm clock and work without it. Rules that react to events (battery, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, screen, headphones, location and so on) need the engine to listen for them.

## Can AutoMate turn Wi-Fi or Bluetooth on and off?
No. Android does not allow normal apps to do that. AutoMate can open the right settings page for you.

## Why did a rule not run?
Open the Activity tab. Skipped rules show which condition failed, and failed steps show the reason. See Troubleshooting for common fixes.

## Is the Accessibility service safe?
It is optional and only presses system buttons when a rule asks. It cannot read your screen. You can switch it off at any time.

## How do I move my rules to another phone?
Use **Backup & restore** to export a file, then import it on the new phone.

## How do I change the language?
Settings > Language.

## Can I use AutoMate on a work or school phone?
Your organisation may block some permissions. Ask your administrator.
