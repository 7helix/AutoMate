# Backup, widget and organising

## Backup and restore
Open **Settings > Backup & restore** (or **Home > Backup**).
- **Export to file** saves all rules as one small file. Keep it in cloud storage or send it to a new phone.
- **Share backup as text** sends the same data through the share sheet.
- **Import from file** reads a backup. You can **add** the rules to your existing ones or **replace** everything.

Only import files you created or trust, because imported rules can run actions on your phone.

## Home-screen widget
1. Touch and hold an empty area of your home screen.
2. Open **Widgets** and find **AutoMate**.
3. Place the widget. It shows whether the engine is running and has a **Start / Stop** button.

## Folders, search and filters
- Give a rule a **folder** in the editor, for example *Work* or *Night*.
- On the Rules tab, **search** by name, folder, trigger or action.
- Filter by **All, Active or Paused**, and by folder.
- Use **duplicate** on a rule card to copy a rule and adjust it.

## Stats
The **Stats** page shows runs this week, your success rate, your day streak, a 7-day chart and your top rules. Counts cover the last 120 days. The "time saved" figure is an estimate of about 10 seconds per run.

## Language
Open **Settings > Language** to switch between English and Hindi. Menus, buttons and the main screens are translated. Articles stay in English for now.
